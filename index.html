How we build

Built to strict standards.

01
Market Thesis
Every strategy begins with a clear behavioural hypothesis about the market — directional bias, trend persistence, or time-of-day dynamics. No curve-fitting.
02
Rigorous Backtesting
Strategies are developed and tested in ProRealTime on IG Markets — with realistic spread, commission assumptions, and backtest periods of 8–16 years.
03
Performance Targets
A strategy must exceed 100% return, deliver strong return relative to its drawdown, and meet minimum Calmar and profit factor thresholds before it is considered for deployment.
04
Automated Execution
Confirmed strategies run fully automatically on IG Markets spread-bet accounts. Entries, exits, stops, and position sizing are all rule-based — zero manual input.
